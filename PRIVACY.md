# Pocket Chef Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 October 2026

Pocket Chef is a recipe app for iPhone, iPad and Mac, made by NeonPixel. It collects no data
about you. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and nothing you do in the
app is sent to NeonPixel.

## What stays with you

- **Your recipes, tags and photos** are stored on your device. If you turn on iCloud sync in
  Settings, they're also stored in your own private iCloud database, which only you can read.
  NeonPixel has no access to it.
- **Recipe capture** (turning typed or pasted text into a recipe) runs on your device with Apple
  Intelligence. The text isn't sent anywhere.
- **Settings**, such as whether you've seen the welcome guide or turned on sync, stay on the
  device.

## When the app goes online

The app only connects to the internet to:

- **Fetch a recipe from a link you paste.** The app downloads that page straight from the
  website, as a browser would. The website sees the visit and can apply its own privacy policy.
  It isn't routed through NeonPixel.
- **Sync through iCloud,** if you turn it on. Apple handles this under
  [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **Download ingredient densities** from Pocket Chef's server, used to convert between cups and
  grams. The request carries no information about you or your recipes, and the server doesn't
  log requests. It holds the address a request came from in memory for about a minute, to stop
  one caller from overloading it. If a request fails on the server, its error log may note that
  address while the failure is diagnosed; the log is deleted by routine rotation.

## Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Recipes synced through iCloud can be
deleted in the app before you delete it, or under Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage
Storage on iPhone and iPad (System Settings → [your name] → iCloud on a Mac).

## Children

Pocket Chef doesn't collect data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be published here with a new date. Its
history is kept in the app's [public repository](https://github.com/neonpixel-software/pocket-chef/commits/main/PRIVACY.md).
