Grandma's Banana Bread (the BEST, trust me!!)

Okay so this is the recipe my grandma made every single Sunday and honestly nothing beats it. You need 3 really ripe bananas, like almost black, 1/3 cup melted butter, 3/4 cup sugar, 1 egg beaten, 1 teaspoon vanilla, 1 teaspoon baking soda, a pinch of salt and 1 1/2 cups of all-purpose flour.

Preheat oven to 350F and butter a 4x8 inch loaf pan. Mash the bananas in a big bowl with a fork. Stir in the melted butter. Mix in the baking soda and salt. Stir in the sugar, beaten egg and vanilla. Mix in the flour. Pour the batter into the pan and bake for about an hour, until a toothpick comes out clean. Let it cool a bit before slicing.

Enjoy!!
