Simple Tomato Soup

Ingredients
2 tbsp olive oil
1 large yellow onion, diced
3 cloves garlic, minced
1 (28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
2 cups vegetable stock
1 tsp sugar
salt and pepper to taste
1/2 cup heavy cream

Instructions
1. Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat.
2. Add the onion and cook until soft, about 8 minutes. Add the garlic and cook 1 minute more.
3. Add a 28 oz can of crushed tomatoes, 2 cups vegetable stock and the sugar. Simmer for 20 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth, stir in the cream and season with salt and pepper.
